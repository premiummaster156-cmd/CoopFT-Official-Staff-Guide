# Head Moderator

**Team:** Low Rank  
**Primary focus:** Lead the moderation function

## Responsibilities
Coordinate Junior Moderators, Moderators, Senior Moderators, and Council; review moderation quality; assign coverage; resolve escalations; and mentor the team.

## How to work
Set priorities, review cases, support difficult decisions, ensure serious reports reach leadership, and identify training needs.

## Boundaries
This role is not expected to personally handle every routine message. Avoid micromanagement and use only granted permissions.

## Professional standards
- Be respectful, calm, fair, and consistent.
- Protect private member and staff information.
- Do not retaliate, show favoritism, or use staff powers for personal disputes.
- Use only permissions assigned to your Discord role.
- Do not invent quotas, promotion promises, or policies that leadership has not announced.

## Escalation
Escalate urgent safety concerns immediately. For routine matters, follow the staff chain of responsibility and involve the relevant Low Rank or High Rank lead. Serious leadership or policy matters go to Owner Rank.

## What good performance looks like
Good performance is a capable moderation team that handles routine work without constant intervention.

This guide describes responsibilities; it does not grant additional Discord permissions.
