# Junior Moderator

**Team:** Mod Roles  
**Primary focus:** Member support and entry-level moderation

## Responsibilities
Help members, watch assigned channels, address low-risk issues, and report rule violations.

## How to work
Review context, remind members of the rule, preserve message links, and refer formal action to a Moderator or higher rank when needed.

## Boundaries
Use only your granted permissions. Do not timeout, kick, ban, change roles, or promise outcomes beyond your authority. Escalate repeated harassment, threats, raids, scams, doxxing, exploitation, and uncertain cases.

## Professional standards
- Be respectful, calm, fair, and consistent.
- Protect private member and staff information.
- Do not retaliate, show favoritism, or use staff powers for personal disputes.
- Use only permissions assigned to your Discord role.
- Do not invent quotas, promotion promises, or policies that leadership has not announced.

## Escalation
Escalate urgent safety concerns immediately. For routine matters, follow the staff chain of responsibility and involve the relevant Low Rank or High Rank lead. Serious leadership or policy matters go to Owner Rank.

## What good performance looks like
Good performance is calm support, reliable reporting, sound judgment, and willingness to learn.

This guide describes responsibilities; it does not grant additional Discord permissions.
