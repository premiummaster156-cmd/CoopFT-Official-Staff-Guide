## Shared standards

- Be fair, calm, respectful, and consistent.
- Review context and evidence before acting.
- Use only permissions assigned to your Discord role.
- Keep reports, staff discussions, applications, and ticket transcripts private.
- Never retaliate, show favoritism, falsify records, or use staff powers for personal disputes.
- Escalate urgent safety matters immediately.
- Do not invent quotas, promotion promises, or policies that leadership has not announced.

## Chain of responsibility

Mod Roles handle routine moderation. Low Rank leads and supports the moderation teams. High Rank primarily oversees Low Rank, coordinates cross-team priorities, and handles senior escalations. High Rank is not expected to carry the same routine moderation workload as moderators.

## Scope

There are intentionally no Owner Rank guides in this repository. Developer responsibilities and custom bot commission procedures are documented in the separate **CoopFT Official Developer Guide**.
